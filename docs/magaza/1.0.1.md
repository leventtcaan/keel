## App Review Information › Notes (1.0'ınkinin yerine)

```
Next Call is a weekly training and nutrition coach. You log workouts, meals and weigh-ins. Once a week a fixed rule engine makes one decision (keep the plan, or change calories, steps or training loads) and shows the reason. No language model makes decisions.

WHAT CHANGED IN 1.0.1: the subscription is no longer required to use the app. Logging training, food and weight is free, and so is the first full weekly call. Later calls show that they are ready and open with a subscription.

SIGN IN: The app uses Sign in with Apple only. There is no other account system, so there is no demo account: sign in with your own Apple ID. The app asks for no name and no email.

SUBSCRIPTION: After signing in, answer the short onboarding questions (units, goal, training days, equipment, consents). The subscription screen is offered at the end and can be closed. It is also reached from Settings > Subscription and from a locked call. It shows the monthly and yearly plans, the free trial for new subscribers, the renewal terms, Restore Purchases, and links to the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy. Purchases are made in the App Store sandbox during review and work with a sandbox tester account.

WEEKLY CALL: A call needs logged data, so a new account shows "No call yet" and says why. The first weekly call appears after the first weekly check-in. Logging works from the first minute.

AI FEATURES ARE NOT ACTIVE in this version. No AI provider is connected and no data is sent to one. The coach answers in fixed wording from the rules.

WALK MAP: Settings > About you > Activity > My walks > Open map. Places the user saves stay on the phone. A typed address and the two points of a walk are sent to Apple Maps to find the place and the walking route; the screen says so. The app reads no location and asks for no location permission.

LANGUAGE: English and Turkish. The app follows the phone's language; Settings > Language changes it.

HEALTH: This is a general wellness app for adults (18+), not a medical device. The Health Notice is at https://leventtcaan.github.io/keel/health/ and is linked from the Terms of Use. Apple Health is read only if the user allows it in the app. Progress photos stay on the phone.

ACCOUNT DELETION (5.1.1(v)): Settings (top of the This week tab) > Delete account. The same account section (export, delete account, sign out) is on the subscription screen.

The server (https://keel-beta.duckdns.org) is running for the review.
```
